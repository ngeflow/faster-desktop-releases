macOS: first-time open

The app isn’t notarized yet, so Gatekeeper will refuse to open it. Two ways past it:

  1. Finder way. Right-click the app in Applications → Open → confirm.
  2. Terminal way. Remove the quarantine flag:
xattr -cr "/Applications/Ngeshare Desktop.app"
